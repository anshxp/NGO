## Component Interactions

### Hero Carousel
- ⏱️ Auto-play: 5 seconds per slide
- ⏸️ Pause: On manual navigation (resumes after 10s)
- ← → Arrow navigation
- ● Dot navigation

### News Widget
- ⏱️ Auto-scroll: 3 seconds per update
- ⏸️ Hover to pause
- ❌ Closeable

### Gallery Carousel
- 🖼️ 6 images with thumbnails
- ← → Arrow navigation
- ● Dot indicators
- 🔍 Click thumbnail to jump

### Floating Buttons
- 💬 WhatsApp: Opens chat
- 🌐 Translate: Language switcher
- 📱 QR Code: Shows modal
